# Ultracode week plan for pyto

Produced 2026-09-09 by a 17-agent workflow (7 subsystem readers, 5 planners with distinct angles, 3 judges, 1 synthesizer, 1 completeness critic) over the checkpoint at commit a4dc559. It is a plan with kill criteria, not a claim of results. Each day is executed by its own multi-agent workflow; every day ends with runnable code, tests the owner can run, and retained evidence committed under `pyto/`. Open judgments are marked `{?}` and are the owner's to resolve.

## Winner and thesis

**Winner.** Experiment-reuse-first (aggregate 62.5/72 across three judges vs Kernel-first 61.5, Replay-first 54.5, Agent-workshop 53.5, JS-parity 37.5), with five grafts: Kernel-first's replay-gated composition promotion (PromotionRefused / provisional / demote+inline reversibility) and fn.pcr.select round trip; Replay-first's fresh-process boundary (`python3 -I`, scrubbed env) plus hidden-state, determinism (PYTHONHASHSEED x CRLF/LF) and false-unchanged auditors and the digest+ref sidecar for non-JSON values; JS-parity's mutation-kill discipline and 'skip with a named reason, never pass silently' rule; Agent-workshop's CAPTURE.md run record, CHANGES.md pairing and provenance lens (every number in the returns resolves to a retained file); and the environment corrections all three judges converged on (isolated `pip wheel` works here while `--no-build-isolation` fails; arxiv unreachable so SUBDUE cites pyto/research/primary-sources.md; disc-stats needs PYTHONPATH=.; consumer tests need data/).

**Thesis.** pyto 0.1.0 at a4dc559 is an address-keyed dict (core.py:46-93) plus an ordered invocation list executed in declaration order (pcr.py:135-177) plus string-ref testimony (pcr.py:59-78); its one distinctive operation is the declaration-time rewrite of a Part binding into the producing invocation's ResultRef (pcr.py:112-116), which is what lets twelve fit/score invocations share one split as 'fn:split'. Everything the docs attribute to reuse validity, replay, comparison and promotion is absent: no digest, no timing, no exporter/importer for uppercase PCR, no traversal, no run identity (docs/PYTHON-LAB-STEWARDSHIP.md:18; pcr.py:74-78). The open {?} InvestigationPayoff judgment (research/pyto-investigation-entrypoint.md:91-105) says the week's value is decided by whether a second experiment reuses the first's grouping, execution shape and comparison procedure with measurable saved work. So the week makes the already-verified synthetic grouped ablation (scratchpad/ablation_spike.py: 5 groups x 3 features, leave-one-group-out, ranking drop_g3 > drop_g0 > drop_g1 recovered in ~23 ms) the spine on Day 1 with zero library changes, then every later day runs a second experiment against it: a re-grouping and re-input with a saved-work ledger and fresh-process replay (Day 2), an ablation of pyto's own primitives by patch plus the conventional stdlib comparison plus replay-gated composition promotion with reversibility (Day 3), a DiscStudio-owned art->Single/Battle fan-out fixture executing stewardship acceptance steps 1-5 and reusing the promoted composition under substitution (Day 4), and an executed SUBDUE substructure replacement with its counterexample, a versioned wheel in a clean venv, one check command and script-generated research returns A-H (Day 5). Library seams are at most one per day, land only after two experiment-local uses, and never change the asdict(tick) shape consumers serialize; retain/replay and promotion code stays experiment-scoped until the owner answers the external-input boundary question. If the procedure transfers with only input Parts changing, that is the first concrete evidence that PxC helps beyond shared function invocation; if it requires re-authoring, the kill criteria record that as the week's counterexample rather than hiding it behind a new primitive.
